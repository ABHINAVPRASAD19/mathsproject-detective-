Reference Guide

  • Conjunctive Simplification:
    P ^ Q → P
  • Disjunctive Amplification:
    P, Q → P v Q
  • Law of Syllogism:
    (P → Q) ^ (Q → R) → (P → R)
  • Modus Ponens:
    P ^ (P → Q) → Q
  • Modus Tollens:
    ~Q ^ (P → Q) → ~P
  • Disjunctive Syllogism:
    (P v Q) ^ ~P → Q
  • Rule of Contradiction:
    (~P → F0) → P
  • Equivalence:
    (P → Q) ≡ (~P v Q)
